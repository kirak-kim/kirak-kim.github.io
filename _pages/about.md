I'm a Ph.D. student at [AIRIS Lab](https://airislab.kaist.ac.kr), KAIST. I work at the intersection of virtual environments and artificial intelligence, with a recent focus on text-to-RIR generation for room impulse responses. Currently a visiting researcher at CARTE, University of Toronto.

I'm interested in how emerging technologies seep into our everyday lives. I make works across research and media art. My current works focus on **audio experience in virtual spaces**.
